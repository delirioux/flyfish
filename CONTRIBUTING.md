# Contributing to Flyfish

Flyfish is a small fork for one piece. Bugs and ideas: [Issues](https://github.com/delirioux/flyfish/issues) on this
repository, please; not Felucca's or Tuna's trackers, since their authors do not maintain Flyfish.

With a bug, say the Flyfish version (the splash, or hold HOME), the steps to reproduce it, what you expected and what
happened. Changes to the notes go in `data/op10no1.txt` (then `python3 tools/ff_etude.py`), with the bar they fix
and the edition you checked them against. `sh tests/ff_tests.sh` must pass.
