# Flyfish: quick cheat sheet

What every control does, by what you want to do with Chopin's Op. 10 No. 1. The [README](../README.md) has the whole
story.

**Getting around:** KNOB 1 to 4 turn the four cards on the screen. A page's button opens it; SEQ steps through
practice's three pages. The keys only ever play music.

## Play it

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| Start / stop | **PLAY**: all 79 bars |
| Tempo / volume | **SELECT** (the score: ♩ = 176) / **MASTER** |
| Go to a bar | **ALGORITHM** (playing: at the next bar line); **OCT-** / **OCT+**: a bar back / on |
| How often round | **ARP**, KNOB 4: **ROUNDS**, INF (for ever, through a seam you won't hear) or a number |
| The sound | **PRESETS**, on any page: GRAND, PLEYEL, TINE EP, HARP, CELESTA, MUSIC BOX, KALIMBA, MARIMBA, SINE KEY |

## Perform it

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| Rubato, dynamics, accents, pedal | **HOME**: **RUBATO**, **TOUCH**, **ACCENT**, **PEDAL** (the score's, HALF, DRY, WASH) |
| A whole reading | **FX**: **STYLE** (CLASSIC, ROMANTIC, VIRTUOSO, NOCTURNE), **LEGATO**, **SOFT** (una corda), **TUNING** |
| Brightness, decay, width | **EDIT**: **SOUND**, **BRIGHT**, **DECAY**, **WIDTH** |
| Mix and room | **GLO**: **RIGHT**, **LEFT** (the hands), **REVERB**, **SPACE** |
| Pitch, colour, score view | Hold **HOME**: MENU, **COLOR**, **LOOK**, **VIEW** (the waterfall or the score), **PITCH** (A = 440, 435, 430) |

## Practise it

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| Slower, a loop, a hand out | **SEQ**: **SPEED** (down to 25 %), **FROM**, **TO**, **YOU** (the hand you play) |
| Loop on / off | **REC** |
| How you play along | **SEQ** again: **MODE** (FREE, TAP: each key the next note, WAIT: the music waits for you), **RAMP**, **CLICK**, **COUNT** |
| Drills | **SEQ** a third time: **RHYTHM** (dotted pairs, accents), **CHORDS** (each group as one chord), **SHOW**, **ERASE** (turn it twice) |
| Your progress | In WAIT, a chart keeps each bar's best clean SPEED and its wrong keys |

## Move it

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| Another key or mode | **ARP**: **KEY**, **MODE** (MINOR, DORIAN, LYDIAN), **BASS** (OCTAVE, SINGLE, DRONE, NONE) |

## Connect

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| MIDI out | The right hand on channel 1, the left on 2, the keys on 3; sustain (CC64) and soft pedal (CC67) on 1 and 2 |
| Saving | Automatic, while stopped |
