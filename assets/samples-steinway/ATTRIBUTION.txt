Changes made for Flyfish: 13 notes (C1, F#1, C2 ... F#6, C7) of velocity layer 12 of 16, from the 44.1 kHz / 16-bit
release; each mixed to mono and cut to its first 3.2 s here (00_Steinway_<note>_v12.wav), then by tools/gen_samples.py
cut by register (2.4 s in the bass to 0.7 s at the top), faded out, resampled to 22.05 kHz, normalised and IMA ADPCM
encoded into the firmware.

  00..12_Steinway_<note>_v12.wav  <-  bitKlavierGrand_PianoBar_44k16b/<note>v12.wav
